    The two worlds. “Low emissions” is SSP1-2.6, where emissions fall sharply. “High emissions” is SSP5-8.5, where they keep rising. “The difference” is high minus low, in percentage points.

    More rain is not less drought. A warmer world also evaporates more water, so a wetter place can still dry out.

    Averages hide storms. These are yearly totals. Heavy downpours can intensify even where the total barely changes.
